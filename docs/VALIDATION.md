## Integration facts

- LG and LL each contain 77,000 rows, six cases and 27 result MM points. `MM_blocks` contains 31 configured points. Four configured 22.8 kV points are not represented in the result files; eight case/MM combinations are missing. They remain missing and do not become zero-valued curves.
- The input result keys are unique by `(Case, MM_name_all, Time (s))`. Numeric data are finite, and `Max_all` matches the maximum of `Max_A`, `Max_B` and `Max_C` within `1e-8`.
- The 04 SA workbook has two curve IDs and 500 source durations per curve, with 500 supplied values per curve. It has four explicit MM assignments. The supplied 0 s point is preserved as source data.
- The updated 05 SA workbook has `SA_Curves` headers `Duration_s` and `66 WTT SA`, 241 supplied curve points, and 40 exact MM assignments to `66 WTT SA`. Its `SA_Types` and `SA_VI_Calculated` sheets are ignored by the app. The 05 integration test confirms SA controls are hidden at 33 kV, appear at 66 kV, and the curve appears on the LG chart for an assigned MM but not on LL.
- Joining those 40 `MM_Assignment.MM_ID` values to `MM_blocks.Group` gives `Un = 66` for all 40 rows, with no unmatched MMs. The SA curve is therefore available at 66 kV; the app does not infer voltage from the curve name or y-values.
- SA spot checks in kV peak: at 0.000 s, `230 OnS SA` = 379.2 and `230 OSS SA` = 381.1; at 0.004 s, 373.6 and 374.8; at 0.998 s, 329.5 and 323.9. No conversion or safety factor is applied to those values.
- For `MM_230_ONS1`, LG and LL use their respective `SIWL=840 kV peak` and `SDPF=460 kV RMS` inputs. The reference values are 840.00 kV peak raw SIWL, 730.43 kV peak SIWL margin, 650.54 kV peak SDPF conversion and 565.68 kV peak SDPF margin. The 0.018 s sample uses SIWL; exactly 0.020 s uses SDPF.
- The full-sample safety-margin comparison has zero LG ranked samples and 444 LL ranked samples above the reference; raw-withstand comparisons have zero for both measurements. These are numerical ranked-index comparisons only, not duration or compliance findings.
- The current second-project dataset has 133,000 rows per CSV, eight cases, 67 result MM points, and 68 configured points in `MM_blocks`. Four voltage levels are available: 33, 66, 230 and 380 kV. One configured MM is absent from the result files, and four case/MM combinations have no curve; no empty curves are generated. The current CSV files contain no exact duplicate rows and no conflicting duplicate curve indices.
